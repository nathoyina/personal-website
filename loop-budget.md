# Loop Budget — personal-website

## Weekly limits

| Loop | Max runs/week | Max tokens/run | Max sub-agent spawns/run |
|------|---------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| Click Triage | 1 | 50k | 0 (L1) |

## On budget exceed

1. Pause weekly triage automation
2. Append event to `loop-run-log.md`
3. Review manually before resuming

## Kill switch

- Flag: `loop-pause-all` in STATE.md
- Resume only after cleared by human
